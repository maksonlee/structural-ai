# Contributing

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [case scope](README.md#scope) before proposing changes. Keep changes focused:
repository/code changes should not silently change engineering assumptions.
Follow [the reproduction guide](docs/development.md) and include actual commands,
verification results and limitations. For design rules, retain traceable source
clauses and update the affected design-stage and workflow-comparison records.
Ordinary article edits stay in WordPress and do not require rerunning the building.

Do not add real client drawings, private logs or third-party manuals without permission.
Do not claim that a passing unit test or solver run certifies a building. A01 is a
simulated case study and the project is still incomplete.

No public contribution policy or overall license has yet been selected by the owner.
Complete the [release review](docs/publishing.md) before soliciting public
contributions or publishing a release.
