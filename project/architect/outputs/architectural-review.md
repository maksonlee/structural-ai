# Architectural proposal and structural handoff

The office proposal combines a regular frame, stairs and elevator, a shared core
wall and entrance reservations. Open the [two review sheets](architectural-review.pdf)
and [core model](core-candidate.ifc). The [requirements](scheme.yaml) and
[core layout](core-proposal.yaml) explain the geometry and finish allowances.

The structural role uses this geometry in the
[analysis review](../../structural/outputs/deliverables/structural-review.md)
and returns [interface drawings](../../structural/outputs/deliverables/structural-drawings.pdf).
Changing a proposal requires review of the affected geometry, loads and outputs.

The main frame includes B2/C2, orthogonal continuous beams and a rear 7.50 m bay.
The side portal starts at y=4.60 m, 0.10 m north of B2's face, with 1.28 m nominal
finished throat. Flights are 1.33 m raw / 1.238 m nominal finished; risers are
133.333 mm and treads 290 mm. Nine dimensional screens and 468 sampled
tread-clearance points are recorded in [the review data](architectural-review.json).

These are scoped geometry screens. All-point protected egress and ground
discharge, rated door products, guards/rails, WC/MEP, certified equipment
reactions, roof service and pit/foundation interfaces remain open. The structural
interface calculation does not provide complete architectural code acceptance.

All drawings are simulated review material. No architect, structural engineer
or contractor has approved a construction package.
