# Taiwan workflow evidence for the article

The article uses a small set of attributable sources to explain the handoff
between architect and structural engineer. It does not claim that one firm,
vendor manual or example establishes a nationwide SOP.

## Practice and roles

Huang Zi-Huan's 2022 interview with structural engineer Yang Xun-Hong of Jian-Ju
describes receiving architectural drawings, preliminary structural decisions,
analysis/calculations, drawings and review responses. Sections 2–3 also discuss
architectural change requests, construction judgement and drafting responsibility.
The account was re-read for the article on 2026-09-13.
[NTU Du Feng issue 173](https://www.ntuce-newsletter.tw/archives/11896).

The register also retains historical firm accounts and firm-reported BIM methods.
Those establish what their authors describe at the stated time, not national
adoption, productivity or numerical equivalence. No new interview was conducted.

## What the case demonstrates

The simulated architect supplies spatial/core inputs. The structural role
checks interpretation and interfaces, uses established calculation tools, and
returns coordinated review sheets. AI assists organization, scripting, source
research and review. The actual H03-C correction and results are in
[the structural response](../../project/structural/outputs/deliverables/structural-review.md).

The [comparison matrix](../../project/comparison/workflow.csv) tracks all major
stages, including incomplete code/RC/foundation/design-drawing work.
The [research gaps](../../project/comparison/research-gaps.yaml) retain what is
not established. For an introductory article, explain those gaps rather than
turning each one into a separate research project.

Cost reduction, contractor acceptance, matched ETABS results and industry labour
savings are unmeasured. Actual process durations are separate from human effort.
The article's [landing illustration](../../project/structural/outputs/deliverables/landing-strip.md)
adds a traced calculation-to-reinforcement step. Official code provisions,
association drawing-note examples, supplier dimensions and synthetic case
choices remain separate evidence categories; no new industry interview is claimed.
Only current reviewed outputs and one full verification remain after cleanup;
old raw paths in dated source/provenance records are not guaranteed available.
